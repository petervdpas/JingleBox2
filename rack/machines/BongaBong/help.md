# BongaBong

A kit. Sixteen pads, one recording to a pad, one pad to a key, all of them able to sound over
each other. Nothing is transposed: a pad plays its recording at the speed it was recorded at,
which is what a kit is and is the whole difference between this and Zampler.

Sixteen pads run from C-4 upwards, so the drawn keyboard at the foot of the machine and a pad box
on your desk reach the same sounds.

## Pads

The grid is the kit. Clicking a pad picks it and the card beside it is about that one. A pad with
nothing on it is silent and says so.

- **Take** is the recording on the pad. **Pick a recording** puts one there off RECORD's shelf.
- **Load samples...** asks for files from anywhere and fills the pads in order, which is how a
  folder of one shot drums becomes a kit in one go.

## Pad

- **Level** is that pad's own, nought to one, under the pattern's volume column and before the
  mixer. It is how a kit is balanced: the snare down a little, the kick left alone.
- **Pan** is where it sits, hard left to hard right. Hats to one side and a tom to the other is
  most of what makes a kit sound like a room rather than a list.
- **Choke** is the group, one to eight, or nought for none. Two pads in the same group cannot
  sound together: the second one silences the first. That is what an open hat and a closed hat
  want, and a triangle mute, and nothing else usually does.

Choke is why this is the one machine on the rack with no New note switch. A kit answers that
question with its groups instead, and it has to: a crash has to be allowed to ring under the
snare that follows it.

## Chop

The same editor Zampler has, and it fills the pads instead of zones. **Pieces** is how many,
**Cut at** is **Hits**, **Gaps** or **Even**, and **Chop** does it. One bar of a break cut into
sixteen and laid across the pads is a kit you can play the break back out of in any order, which
is what this is really for.

## What ships with it

Four kits: Live Drums, Tribalcore Kicks, Dirty Old-School and Blue Boat Drums, each with its own
recordings in the machine's own folder, so they travel in the zip and are on every installation
that has the machine.

A preset here is which recordings sit on which pads and what each pad is set to. Picking one
replaces the kit; the pads you filled yourself are yours until you do.
