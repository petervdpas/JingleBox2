# Mixer

Which parts of the mixer are switched on.

Each part here can be switched off on a machine with little to spare, such as a
Raspberry Pi. Off means the part is not shown **and** its work is not done, both. A
switch takes effect at once and is kept for the next start. Nothing in a song is changed
by it, so a song saved with a part off opens with that part whole on a machine that has
it on.

- **Song automation** is the automation strip on the mixer: levels, pans, mutes, the
  tempo and the song's effects over the whole song. Off, the strip is gone and the song's
  lanes are kept but neither played nor recorded, so the song plays at the tempo it is
  set to and every fader stays where it is.
- **Side chain** is the Duck from row at the foot of each track's strip. Off, the row is
  gone and no track is ducked under another, so nothing is worked out for it while the
  song plays. A song that ducks plays without it until it is switched on again.
- **Patchbay** is the tab beside the desk. Off, the tab is gone and its picture is
  neither built nor kept moving. The sound still goes where it is patched: the patching
  is where the audio goes, and only the picture of it is switched off.
